# Project Overview

The CM Kerala Public Grievance Redressal Portal is a digital channel for citizens to submit public-service grievances and for government offices to review, route, investigate, and resolve them. It brings citizens, the Chief Minister's Office (CMO), and department officers together in a role-based workflow, with reference numbers and status history to make progress easier to follow.

## Problem Statement

Citizens who need to report a public issue may have to travel to government or CMO offices, navigate in-person processes, and follow up without a convenient way to see what is happening. This creates a barrier to access and can make it difficult for the responsible office to receive supporting information and keep citizens informed.

The portal provides a virtual channel for citizens to raise grievances without making those trips. Citizens can describe an issue, attach supporting evidence, and follow its progress using a reference number. CMO staff can review and route each grievance to the appropriate department, while department officers can record the action taken. This supports a more accessible, traceable, and accountable grievance-handling process.

## Objectives

- Make it easier for citizens to submit public grievances online.
- Reduce the need for in-person travel solely to file or follow up on a grievance.
- Allow citizens to provide relevant supporting documents or other permitted files with a submission.
- Help CMO staff review, prioritize, and assign grievances to responsible departments.
- Give department officers a clear channel to report actions taken.
- Provide role-appropriate status visibility, grievance history, remarks, and resolution information.
- Collect citizen feedback after resolution to help assess the service experience.
- Support administrative oversight through dashboards and grievance summaries.

## Scope

### In scope

- Citizen registration and sign-in, grievance submission, and access to grievances submitted by the signed-in citizen.
- Optional file attachments to grievances, action-taken reports, and supplemental grievance records, subject to configured upload limits.
- Reference-number-based grievance identification and role-authorized tracking.
- CMO review, prioritization, department and officer assignment, escalation, remarks, and resolution.
- Department officer access to grievances assigned to their department and submission of action-taken reports.
- Grievance status and assignment history, citizen feedback after resolution, and dashboards for CMO and department users.
- Public information pages, a public department listing, and a contact enquiry form.

### Out of scope

- Replacing existing government departments, their decision-making authority, or their official investigation processes.
- Guaranteeing a particular resolution time or outcome for any grievance.
- Anonymous grievance submission; accounts are required for grievance submission and owner-specific tracking.
- Handling emergency services or urgent safety incidents in place of the appropriate emergency channels.
- Treating the portal as a general-purpose application for requests unrelated to public grievances.

## Key Features

### Citizen portal

- Register and sign in to a citizen account.
- Submit a grievance with issue details and optional supporting attachments.
- Receive a grievance reference number and view the status and history of submitted cases.
- Review CMO remarks and resolution information.
- Submit one feedback rating after a grievance has been resolved.
- Send a general enquiry through the contact form.

### CMO portal

- View grievance dashboards and role-authorized grievance lists.
- Review submitted grievances, assign a department and officer, and set priority or an optional due date.
- Escalate open grievances and add public or internal remarks.
- Review department action reports and record a final resolution or rejection.
- Manage departments and department officers, and review contact enquiries.

### Department portal

- View department workload and grievances assigned to the department.
- Review grievance details and submit action-taken reports, with optional supporting attachments.
- Follow the grievance lifecycle through its recorded status and history.

### Grievance lifecycle

Grievances move through the following statuses as they are handled:

`SUBMITTED` → `UNDER_REVIEW` → `IN_PROGRESS` → `RESOLVED`

A grievance may instead be marked `REJECTED` following review. The portal records status changes and assignment history to support traceability.
