# User Guide

This guide explains how citizens, Chief Minister's Office (CMO) administrators, and department officers use the grievance portal. Each role has a separate sign-in page and role-specific access.

## Getting started

Use the public website to read about the service, view departments, and access the citizen portal. Sign in through the page for your role:

- **Citizen:** create an account or sign in from the Citizen portal.
- **CMO administrator:** sign in from the CMO portal using the account provisioned by the system administrator.
- **Department officer:** sign in from the Department portal using an account created by a CMO administrator.

Citizen registration creates citizen accounts only. It does not provide CMO or officer access. When you are finished, select **Logout** to end your session.

## For citizens

### Create an account or sign in

1. Choose **Register** in the Citizen portal.
2. Enter your full name, mobile number, email address, and password, then confirm your password.
3. After successful registration, the portal signs you in and opens your dashboard.
4. If you already have an account, choose **Login** and enter your email and password.

### Submit a grievance

1. From your dashboard, choose **Submit grievance**.
2. Select the district and category that best describe the issue.
3. Enter a concise subject and a detailed description. Include relevant location and timing information so the responsible office can understand the issue.
4. Optionally attach supporting images or documents. The portal accepts JPEG, PNG, WebP, PDF, and Word files; up to five files can be submitted at once, with a 10 MB limit per file.
5. Select **Submit grievance**. The portal confirms submission and displays a reference number. Keep this number for follow-up.

### View and track your grievances

- The dashboard shows your submitted grievance count, pending and resolved counts, and a list of recent submissions with their reference, subject, status, and date.
- Select **Track grievance** and enter the reference number to view the grievance status, assigned department, priority, resolution (when available), and status history.
- Sign in with the citizen account that submitted the grievance. Grievances are private to their submitting account; a reference number alone does not grant access.
- The track page opens automatically after submission with the new reference number filled in.

### Review a resolution and leave feedback

1. After a grievance is resolved, open **Updates and feedback**.
2. Choose the resolved grievance from the available list.
3. Select a rating from 1 to 5 and optionally enter a comment.
4. Submit feedback. Only one feedback submission is accepted per grievance.

### Contact the portal team

Use the public **Contact** page to send a general enquiry by entering your name, email, subject, and message. This form is for enquiries, not for submitting or tracking a grievance.

## For CMO administrators

CMO accounts are provisioned by the system administrator. Public registration cannot create a CMO account.

### Review the dashboard

After signing in, use the dashboard to review total, pending, resolved, and overdue grievance counts, along with district, department, and status summaries.

### Review and filter grievances

1. Open **All Grievances**.
2. Narrow the list by status, category, priority, or district.
3. Search by reference number within the displayed page.
4. Choose **View record** to inspect the grievance details, citizen information, attachments, assignment and status histories, remarks, and department action reports.

### Assign a grievance to a department

1. Open a grievance that has the `SUBMITTED` status.
2. In **Assign department**, select an active department.
3. Optionally select an officer from that department.
4. Set a priority and, if needed, a due date.
5. Select **Assign grievance**.

The grievance moves to `UNDER_REVIEW`. Its assignment and status changes are recorded in the histories. Use **Escalate grievance** when an open case needs escalation; resolved or rejected cases cannot be escalated.

### Review department action and resolve a grievance

1. On the grievance detail page, review the department's action report and any supporting attachments.
2. Add a **Public remark** to communicate an update to the citizen.
3. Enter resolution details and select **Resolve grievance**.

The grievance must be `IN_PROGRESS` and have a public CMO remark before it can be resolved. Once resolved, the status and resolution are visible to the citizen, who can submit feedback.

### Manage departments and officers

- Open **Departments** to create a department with a name and description. You may assign an available, unassigned officer while creating it.
- The same page lets you select a department and assign an unassigned officer to it.
- Open **Administration** and use **Create department officer** to register an officer with their name, mobile number, email, and an initial password. You may assign an active department at creation or leave the officer unassigned.
- Use a strong initial password and share account credentials securely with the officer. Officers cannot be registered through public citizen registration.

### Handle contact enquiries

Open **Administration** to review public contact enquiries. Mark an enquiry as **Reviewed** or **Close** it when appropriate.

## For department officers

Department officers sign in using credentials supplied by the CMO administrator. An officer must be assigned to a department to access its grievance workload.

### Review assigned cases

1. Sign in through the Department portal.
2. Use the dashboard to view department workload counts.
3. Open **Assigned Cases** to see grievances assigned to your department.
4. Select **View details** to read the issue, status, priority, attachments, previous action reports, and status history.

Officers can access only grievances assigned to their department. If the expected case is missing, contact the CMO administrator to confirm the assignment and department account.

### Investigate and report action taken

1. Review the grievance details and any citizen attachments.
2. Investigate the issue through the department's normal process.
3. On the grievance detail page, enter an action report describing the investigation and actions taken. The report must contain at least 10 characters.
4. Optionally attach up to five supporting files (JPEG, PNG, WebP, PDF, or Word documents; maximum 10 MB each).
5. Select **Submit action report**.

The report is sent to the CMO for review. The first action report moves a grievance from `UNDER_REVIEW` to `IN_PROGRESS`; further reports may be submitted while it remains in progress.

Officers may also upload supplemental attachments to an accessible grievance. Use the attachment download links to review existing grievance or report files. CMO administrators handle final resolution and citizen-facing remarks.

## Understanding grievance statuses

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `SUBMITTED` | A citizen has filed the grievance; it is awaiting CMO review and assignment. |
| `UNDER_REVIEW` | CMO has assigned it to a department for review or action. |
| `IN_PROGRESS` | Department action has been reported; CMO review and resolution are pending. |
| `RESOLVED` | CMO has recorded the resolution. The citizen may submit feedback. |
| `REJECTED` | The grievance was rejected during review. |

## Safe and effective use

- Describe the issue clearly and provide accurate contact information.
- Attach only files relevant to the grievance; avoid including unnecessary sensitive personal information.
- Keep your account password private and log out when using a shared device.
- Do not use the portal as a substitute for emergency services or urgent safety reporting.
- A reference number helps identify a case but does not bypass sign-in or role-based access controls.
